# Progression Gate 3: Production Engineering & Capstone Defense (Week 24)

**Gate Milestone:** Final Graduation & Junior DevOps Engineering Certification  
**Format:** Capstone Architecture Presentation (45 mins) + Chaos Injection Drill (60 mins) + Comprehensive Technical Examination (45 mins)  
**Passing Threshold:** Minimum 85 / 100 aggregate score. Unanimous mentor sign-off.

---

## 1. Mandatory Competencies Checklist
- [ ] **Kubernetes Operations:** Workload management, Deployments, Services, Ingress, HPA, Helm charts, and cluster troubleshooting.
- [ ] **Observability:** Full telemetry stack (Prometheus, Grafana, Node Exporter), PromQL alerting rules, and incident post-mortems.
- [ ] **DevSecOps:** Automated Trivy container scanning, IAM least privilege, TLS/SSL automation, and dependency auditing.
- [ ] **SRE & Production Operations:** High availability design, automated backup/restore drills, disaster recovery planning (RTO/RPO), and chaos engineering.
- [ ] **Capstone Delivery:** A fully operational, production-grade 3-tier cloud deployment provisioned via IaC and CI/CD.

---

## 2. Gate 3 Final Defense
1. **Capstone Architecture Defense:** Defending the entire Capstone system before a panel of mentors.
2. **Chaos Drill:** Triage and mitigation of an active injected production outage.
3. **Comprehensive Technical Exam:** Written examination covering 24 weeks of engineering principles.
